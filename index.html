How it works

Everything runs in this tab. Nothing is uploaded, and the logs are only ever read.

  1. ParseEVTX chunks, Binary XML templates and substitutions decoded by hand. Tested record-for-record against python-evtx.
  2. NormalizeSecurity, System, Sysmon, PowerShell, Defender, RDP and Task Scheduler events become one timeline.
  3. DetectSingle-event rules plus correlations over time: brute force → success, spray, Kerberoast bursts, backdoor admins.
  4. ExplainRisk score, attack story, ATT&CK matrix, entities, Markdown report and ATT&CK Navigator layer.
